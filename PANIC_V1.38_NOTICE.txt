PANIC / panic.x V1.38 notice
===========================

Build 6.12c embeds the same original panic.x V1.38 binary previously used by
PanicPlayerTab5 so PANIC data can be launched from the unified Tab5 GUI.

written by Hideya Nagata (pako / ぱこたん / 永田英哉), with panic.x V1.38 based on
V1.34 and modified by Nashimi (なしみ).  Copyright in PANIC remains with
Hideya Nagata (pako).  The original distribution documentation should be kept
with public redistributions and remains the authority for its terms.

Individual .PAN data files are separate works; their redistribution terms
